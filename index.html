Prioritised recommendations

PriorityActionExpected impact
1Cap discounts at 20%, with manager approval above itUp to +$220,214.46 profit (margin 12.6% → 20.1%); at a 40% cap, +$90,600.79
2Reprice or restrict discounts on Tables, Bookcases and MachinesRemoves about $71,640 of losses on discounted lines (Tables −$30,790, Machines −$29,555, Bookcases −$11,295)
3Review Central region and TX, OH, PA, IL pricing and discount approvalThese four states lost $70,869; other regions earn 11.9–15.0% margin
4Delist or reprice the 20 products that lose money on every saleStops repeat leakage on lines such as Bevis conference tables and O'Sullivan bookcases
5Protect and promote stars: Copiers, Accessories, Paper, LabelsThese have 25–44% margins and support the 81.6% profit concentration in top products

Caveats

  • The cap simulation holds volume constant; actual results depend on customer response.
  • Currency is assumed to be USD; Canadian rows (200 lines, about 1% of sales) may differ.
  • The file does not match the described 319-row, Jan–Apr 2023 scope; confirm the intended dataset.
  • Two duplicate-looking line items were kept; impact is under $400 of sales.
